Wow. I feel a whole lot better for having written all of that out. Now I should
say, this is a non-exhaustive list and some of these are higher priority than
others. I should also note we clearly fall into an anti-pattern here, because
we've actually priced up a surprising number of those jobs. However, we either
wince at how expensive they are and decide against it for now, or we start
trying to get other quotations and get derailed, or we say "yes" and then the
contractor doesn't come or can't do the job.

Is it any wonder we're exhausted? This isn't even the priority. Three small
children, full-time work, a part-time PhD, maintaining health, fitness, and a
social life...

I guess we just have to work through them, step-by-step. I know we have what
could be a beautiful house, but it's frustrating not being able to see that
through the jobs. Knowing that we've probably got a few years and tens of
thousands of pounds until the house is how we want it.
