Today has been more apple-heavy than any day ever should be.<!--more--> Not
Apple. I mean, I'm writing this on an Apple laptop but no...real apples from
real trees. Garden of Eden and Eve. Newton and gravity. That kind.

We've got a pretty big apple tree in the garden and it's mainly been the bane
of my life. It drops **so many apples** every autumn and my poor little robot
lawnmower can't handle them and gives up. At least once a day I get a
notification on my phone that he's stuck, and I trudge up in the rain to kick
apples into the borders and get him started again.

This year looked like it'd be no different but I chatted to some of my AI
friends and asked them what I could do with all the apples. As usual, they
suggested a number of things that I'm definitely not going to do. The
assumption they all have is that I'm awash with time and would like nothing
better than to spend a day peeling and coring apples.

I abused an LLM enough until it told me that I can just quarter them, lob them
in a slow cooker, and then strain them through a sieve and Bob's your job. I had
the one-year-old at a loose end this morning and he wanted to go outside and
there were apples everywhere so I gave it a go.

Firstly, a good round of 50 or so apples kicked into the long grass. Then,
shake the tree to make sure you get fresh ones. Side note: the one-year-old
finds it hilarious when all the apples fall on the ground which already made
the whole thing worth it. He also finds it funny when you throw them all in the
buckets which is, coincidentally, the next step.

Then after using every pot and pan I own and boiling apples until the kitchen
smelled like an orchard, I made more apple puree than I've ever seen in my
life. At least enough for 16 apple crumbles. Comfortably. And that was using
around a quarter of the apples that I made fall off the tree in one day.

It's a big apple tree.

Anyway, then my mother-in-law used my apple puree to make an apple crumble with
the big two children. As an afternoon snack, we all had a little bit of apple
crumble made from the apples that had, this morning, been sitting on the tree
in our garden. I just had another portion with ice-cream and it was brilliant.

Now I need to start up pork and apple stew, make an apple
chutney to go with cheeses, mix some apple through porridge or yoghurt for
breakfast. We cannot rest. We _must_ eat more apples than we've ever eaten
before.

Apples.
