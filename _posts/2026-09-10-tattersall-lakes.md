I disappeared for about a week and want to share what I did, where I went, and
how it was.<!--more-->

## Key Details 

Tattershall Lakes. Away Resorts. _"Luxury"_ caravan. 4 nights. My parents in a
"Burst of Blue" with the eldest two. My wife, my 1-year-old and I in the
caravan.

## Colour

Overall, it was pretty fun.

My one-year-old was (and is) a nightmare of chaos and had a lot of fun
discovering a new pan draw, emptying out a new bin, and turning on a new gas
hob. He had a good time in the outdoor lido (except when it was too windy) and
the splash park (except when we wouldn't let him climb up the slide).

My four-year-old **loved** the hot tub and is now telling everybody she meets
that the hot tub was the best bit of her holiday. She also did pottery painting
and made a unicorn money box, and made a friend at the splash park.

My seven-year-old got to do an inflatable obstacle course **on** the lake with
wetsuits and flotation aids, and had his first ever Dominos. He also thought
the splash park was pretty good.

We played crazy golf and went to Tattershall castle. We watched countless
planes come in and out of RAF Coningsby. And my wife and I had many a deep chat
in the hot tub once the baby was asleep, and played Stratego and Jaipur and
generally revelled in not having a TV or laptops.

I read something a while ago that kids love _all_ holidays and the reason you
spend money is to make the experience more pleasant for the parents. I think
that's probably right. This was an unashamedly _cheap_ holiday and the kids
wouldn't know it in the slightest. Us, banging around in a small caravan under
a noisy flight path...we did.

Lots of fun - I hope the kids will remember it and when I asked them they said
it was the best ever.
