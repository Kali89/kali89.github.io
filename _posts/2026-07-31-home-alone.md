I'm home alone!<!--more--> My wife has taken the incredibly bold step of
driving *all three* of our children up to Scotland by herself to spend some
time with her parents. Not just some time, though. 6 - 7 days. Nearly a whole
week!

I shouldn't need to say it but I will anyway...what a *hero*! Imagine...a
1-year-old who believes chaos is his calling card. A 4-year-old who can strop
with the best of them and will be high on sugar from minute one. And the
noisiest 6-year-old yet invented.

What will I be doing with my freedom? Well, so far it looks like it's going to
be pretty gentle. I ghost around the eerily silent house. Why bother turning
lights on if it's just me?

So far I've done a fair amount of work, a little bit of PhD, a little bit of
writing, a tiny bit of guitar, and a bit of tidying. I've made poor dietary
decisions, and been to the shop to lock in yet more poor dietary decisions.
I've been to the gym to maintain a sense of normality. But that's just been
Thursday and half of Friday. The weekend is likely to be quieter still.

I thought I'd go to bed late and get up late...and I was half right. For some
inexplicable reason, I was up at 5:40 and again at 7. Well...maybe it's not
inexplicable. Maybe I'm old and broken now...too many mornings of early waking
have turned me (unwillingly, I should add) into a morning person. By 8am I'd
showered, breakfasted and was working.

On at least one of the days, I want to do something that I otherwise
wouldn't/couldn't. At the moment my best idea is to go to the cinema but I'm
open to finding inspiration from elsewhere. On another day, I want to see if I
can go the whole day without speaking once.

At the pub again for a bit of outside working and the beef brisket sandwich is
delightful. Ta ta.
