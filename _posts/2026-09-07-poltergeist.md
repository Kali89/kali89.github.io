My wife recently shared a theory that I can't shift. My one-year-old is a
poltergeist.<!--more-->

For a second, let's just consider what poltergeists actually **do**. Sure,
they're invisible and that's their whole _thing_, but if you ignore that? They
float around causing chaos, making loud noises, and re-arranging furniture for
no other reason than increasing entropy.

As my one-year-old crawls around the floor, shouting loudly, banging pan lids
and fucking shit up...I see it. Maybe he was a poltergeist in a former life.
Maybe he imagines he's invisible. I don't know.

All I know is that he lives his life like the world is just one big thing that
absolutely needs fucking up **right now**.
