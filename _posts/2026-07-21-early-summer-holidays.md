It's day 2 of the summer holidays and I think it's going to be a long
one!<!--more--> We're still working out how to handle parenting and being back
at work with 3 children off school/nursery and at home all day. It's almost
entirely falling to my wife and she's started off strong: the kids are doing
reading, maths, have been to Piglets for the day and are having a great time.
However, it's clearly **a lot** of work and I don't think she'll be able to
keep up with it.

I'm mainly shutting myself away in the annexe and working a crazy amount of
hard - lots of LLM-assisted data work, none of which really gives me the time
to gather.

At some point, we'll find a new balance but as the activities continue
(swimming and taekwondo aren't stopping during the summer, and dancing is still
on for a week or two) and the work piles up, I'm aware that my wife is likely
to reach breaking point sooner or later.

Next week there's tennis camp which might help (taking 2 children out of the
equation for a few hours) but then a mega-trip up to her parents with all the
children for up to 5 days. Pray for her, dear reader. Pray for her.

I have to decide whether to do something outlandish (fly to New
York/Pisa/somewhere for a week), slightly landish (train to Oxford, go back to
Chesterfield), or perfectly vanilla (maybe get a takeaway one night). For now,
I haven't planned anything so I'm tending towards the latter but you never
know.

Let this serve as a reminder - the 49 days of the summer holidays started well
and frantically, and I'm impressed with my wife's ability to handle it. Long
may it continue.
