## It's good that they're smiling

My six-year-old son, no doubt encouraged by his metal-loving father, is really
into power metal. Swords ringing, dragon slaying, battle pumping power metal.
My wife is generally bemused but accepting: you parent the child you have, and our child
loves Dragonforce and Majestica. In fairness, she spent at least some time at
University watching me play rock in various bands so this is sort of on her...

My son and I were watching the video above (Diggy Diggy Hole by Wind Rose) and my wife
was surprised (but happy) to find that the band were all smiling and laughing
while playing. It's something that's sat with me, and I think I now
understand why.

New listeners to metal tend to imagine angry serious men playing angry
serious songs, and sometimes that's true. Sometimes, dudes _are_ angry and
that's OK.
 
But sometimes, a group of men who have known each other for years and become
masters of their instruments get together and have fun. They write fun songs that sound cool. The songs lets you imagine yourself as a band of brothers
going on a quest. You come together and achieve something greater than the sum
of your parts. You make something. And then, you go on stage in front of
thousands of people and shout _"are you ready, brothers? Will you fight with
me?"_ and they all scream with you. Fun? What could be funner?

So yes, the band are all smiling, and so am I. My son loves it, too, and it was
great to see my wife start to realise why.
