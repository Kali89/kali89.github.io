With Halloween comes Trick or Treating---clearly one of the best night of the
year for young kids.<!--more-->

Dressing up. Tick.
Wandering the street with their friends after bedtime. Tick.
Getting given sweets and chocolate. Tick.

Ours had a great time, and for the first time we teamed up with another family
which was a nice change of pace.

There are little 'kid-isms'---phrases or words that kids haven't quite mastered
that lead to delightful misunderstandings. As a kid, for the longest time, I'm
led to believe I conflated "lie-in" with "lion" and so would comment on my
parents having a lion.

My four-year-old daughter is convinced that Trick or Treating is actually
called Trickle Treating and I love it.
